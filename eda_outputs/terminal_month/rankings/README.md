# Terminal position summaries

Generated by `make_terminal_position_summaries` in `scripts/analysis/terminal_month_analysis.py` for role-specific PageRank, terminal and country counterparty HHI, and role-specific dependence.

- `terminal_position_summary.csv`: one row per terminal, observed active role and metric. Reports the mean, median, minimum and maximum in valid active role-months, all months attaining the maximum, activity frequency, mean monthly rank and frequency in the top 20.
- `full_period_top_mean_positions_with_ties.csv`: highest active-month means within each role and metric, including ties at rank 20.
- `monthly_position_ranks.csv`: all valid active observations, ranked within month, observed role and metric.
- `monthly_top_positions_with_ties.csv`: monthly competition ranks at most 20. All cutoff ties are included, so there may be more than 20 rows in a group.
- `monthly_top_terminals.csv`: existing top-20 output, now also covering country HHI. This legacy file still selects at most 20 observations; use the new tie-aware file for interpretation.

These summarise scores calculated separately on each monthly network; they do not recompute metrics on an aggregated network. Means are unweighted across months. Dependence is compared separately by observed role because exporter and importer values measure different relationships. A terminal changing role has separate summary rows.

`active_share` uses all panel months. `top_n_share_valid_role_months` conditions on months with an observed score in that role; `top_n_share_panel_months` uses all panel months. Inspect `eligible_terminals`: inclusion in the top 20 is less selective in small groups. High HHI means concentration, not necessarily high criticality.

Only PageRank has `mean_pagerank_all_months_zero_outside_role`: sum of observed PageRank in that role divided by all panel months, treating inactivity and other-role months as zero. For terminals with a constant active role, this is the mean across the whole panel with inactive months set to zero. It is left missing if active role-month scores are missing. HHI and dependence remain undefined outside active role-months.

The checked-in summaries were generated from the existing terminal-month CSV. Country metadata was joined from `eda_outputs/node_month/data/node_month_metrics.csv`, since the previous terminal CSV omitted country. Future full script runs include country directly. The underlying voyage workbook is not tracked here, so the complete voyage reconstruction was not rerun.
